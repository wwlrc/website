Wye Valley & Forest of Dean · since 1987

Land rovering in the Wye Valley and the border country

Set up in July 1987 by a group of Land Rover owners around the Wye Valley and the Forest of Dean. There are over a hundred of us now, from Hereford down towards Gloucester and all points west.

We run an event most months, usually the third Sunday. You’re very welcome to come and watch — it’s free and there’s nothing to book.
