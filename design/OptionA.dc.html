New to all this?

Come and watch, free

Turn up at any event, have a look round and ask us anything. No membership needed to spectate.

Membership from £1

Full £30 a year, family and associate £10, juniors and children £1. Apply online or by post.

Five disciplines

Tyro, road-taxed vehicle trials, cross-country, time trials and competitive safari.
