Five ways to have a go

Whatever you drive — a brand new Defender or a Series with not a straight panel in sight — there’s something for you.

Tyro

Showroom-standard vehicles, completely non-damaging. Thirteen-year-olds can drive.

RTV trial

Road-legal vehicles against each other. Meant to be non-damaging.

Cross country

Specialist vehicles, much harder, and it can get expensive.

Time trial

Cross country, but against a target time. A penalty for every second over.

Comp safari

A cross-country race against the clock. The fast, loud one.
