A Land Rover club for the Wye Valley and the borders

Set up in July 1987 around the Wye Valley and the Forest of Dean, and over a hundred members now, from Hereford down towards Gloucester and all points west. We run an event most months, usually the third Sunday, and you’re welcome to come and watch any of them for nothing.
